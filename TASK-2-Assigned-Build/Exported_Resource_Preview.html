The Pre-Submission Paper Checklist

A simple, pre-flight checklist designed to catch mismatched numbers and missing data before you submit your paper to Nature, IEEE, or a grant committee.

Why we built this

When multiple co-authors work on a 40-page draft, mistakes happen. You might update a learning rate in Section 3, but forget to update it in Section 6. Missing a simple Data Availability Statement can delay your paper for weeks. We built this checklist to stress-test your manuscript and catch these errors before the reviewers do.

1. Abstract Checker

Ensures every claim in your abstract matches the actual data and tables in your results section.

2. Number Tracker

Makes sure your numbers (like sample sizes and test scores) match perfectly across every single section of the paper.

3. Compliance Check

Reminds you to include required data links and ethics approvals so your paper isn't rejected on day one.

4. Reviewer Prep

A quick test to make sure you have answered common questions reviewers always ask (like reproducibility and fairness).

The Master Template

Copy the raw markdown template below and paste it directly into your workspace (Notion, Obsidian, Overleaf, or SuperDocs). Fill it out systematically before your final PDF compile.
