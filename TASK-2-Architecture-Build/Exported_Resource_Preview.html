The Liability Gap

In high-stakes architecture, document drift is the leading cause of contractor disputes.

If a client changes a material spec from Brick to Pre-cast Concrete in the Design Narrative, but that change is not manually synced to the Structural Load calculations or the Final Budget Sheet, the General Contractor will build the wrong spec—and your firm assumes the liability.
